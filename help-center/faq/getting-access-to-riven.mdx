## Overview

To use Riven, sign up for an account and verify your email address.

## Steps

1. Visit `https://riven.ai/signup`
2. Fill in your details and submit
3. Check your inbox for the verification email
4. Click the link to activate your account
5. Log in at `https://riven.ai` and start building

<Callout kind="info">
Trouble signing up? Contact support at support@riven.ai.
</Callout>
